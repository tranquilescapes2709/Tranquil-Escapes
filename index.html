Meet Your Property Partner

Hi, I'm Erin Carter — Founder of Tranquil Escapes Property Management

I started Tranquil Escapes Property Management because I believe that owning property should be a source of peace, not stress. Whether you're a homeowner, an investor, or a lender with a property in need of care, I'm here to make sure your investment is protected and thriving.

Based in Philadelphia, PA, I bring a hands-on, professional approach to every property I manage and every client I serve. I take pride in treating each property as if it were my own — with attention to detail, proactive communication, and a genuine commitment to excellence.

In addition to property management and preservation services, I also help families and individuals plan unforgettable vacation experiences. Because at the end of the day, life is about more than just work — it's about creating memories and living fully.

My mission is simple: to give you back your time, protect your investment, and open the door to new experiences. I'm not just your property manager — I'm your partner in building the life you deserve.

Property Preservation

Protecting your investment at every stage

Safeguarding Properties with Precision & Care

Property preservation is about protecting what matters — whether it's a vacant home, a bank-owned property, or a foreclosure in need of attention. We work with homeowners, lenders, and asset management companies to maintain properties to the highest industry standards.

❄️

Winterization

Complete winterization services to protect plumbing, heating systems, and the structure of vacant properties during cold months.

🔒

Securing & Lock Changes

Boarding of windows and doors, lock changes, and re-keying to secure vacant or foreclosed properties from unauthorized entry.

🌿

Lawn Care & Debris Removal

Regular grass cutting, weed removal, leaf cleanup, and debris hauling to keep properties in compliance and curb appeal high.

🏚️

Initial Secure & Convey

First-time property securing including full inspections, photo documentation, and completion of all required lender reports.

🔍

Property Inspections & Reports

Routine occupancy checks, interior and exterior inspections, and detailed photo reports delivered to lenders and asset managers.

🛠️

Rehab & Repairs Coordination

Minor to major repair coordination using licensed contractors, from cosmetic updates to full property rehabilitation projects.

🧹

Interior Cleaning & Trash Out

Complete interior cleanouts, junk removal, and deep cleaning services to prepare properties for sale or re-occupancy.

📋

Lender Compliance

Full compliance with HUD, Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, and private lender guidelines for property condition and maintenance standards.
